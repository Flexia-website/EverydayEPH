DOBO

Nigerian Rubber Band Game

HOW TO PLAY

  • Each player starts with 5 rubber bands.
  • Take turns shooting one band onto the field.
  • If your band lands overlapping an opponent's band, you capture it!
  • Captured bands are added to your inventory.
  • Run out of bands on your turn and you lose.
